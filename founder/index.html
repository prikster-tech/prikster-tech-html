Founder, Prikster Tech

I’m a senior software engineer with over a decade of experience — most of it building the tools that thousands of US home-service and construction businesses run on every day: scheduling, invoicing, payments, and the systems that keep a service company moving.

That experience left me with a conviction: much of the software small businesses depend on is harder to use than it should be. Prikster Tech is the company I’m establishing to act on that — building products that rethink outdated tools from the ground up, starting with the trades and service industries I know best.

As the company takes shape, I’m also planning to work directly with a small number of businesses on their software and on practical uses of AI — the kind that saves hours, not the kind that makes headlines.
